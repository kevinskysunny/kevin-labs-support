HarmonyOS on your Mac.

Connect a HarmonyOS NEXT phone over USB. View its screen, navigate and type from your Mac.

Apple Silicon only · macOS 12 or later

Trial and purchase coming soon

Contact support

What you can do

View the phone screen

Follow your phone’s screen in a desktop window. Refresh speed depends on your phone and connection.

Navigate from your Mac

Click, swipe, return home and go back with desktop controls.

Type and copy text

Use your Mac input method and transfer plain text between clipboards. Phone system support varies.

Before connecting

Use a data-capable USB cable. Enable developer mode and USB debugging on the phone, then approve this Mac. HDC is bundled; DevEco Studio is not required.

Compatibility

This release targets Apple Silicon Macs. Phone models and HarmonyOS versions are being verified. Audio forwarding, wireless mirroring and Intel Macs are not currently offered.

Purchase and activation

Planned: Paddle one-time purchase for version 1.x and its updates, two Macs, 14-day trial without a card. License activation requires internet; a valid signed token allows up to 30 days offline. Sales open after activation is ready.

Support

Privacy

Screen images and clipboard text are processed locally via HDC. The current capture implementation writes temporary JPEG files on the Mac and phone; files may remain after exit. License activation and purchases will use online services; their final policy will be published before sales.
