Frequently Asked Questions

Where is BarNest located?

BarNest lives directly in your menu bar. Look for the nest divider icon (< or >) in your status bar. Click it to expand or collapse hidden icons, or right-click to open Settings.

Why does BarNest ask for Accessibility permissions?

macOS requires Accessibility permissions to detect the positions of status bar icons and provide notch-safe folding. BarNest runs 100% locally on your device and never records keystrokes or screen contents.

How does the 7-day free trial and lifetime license work?

BarNest gives you a full-featured 7-day free trial upon first launch. After 7 days, you can unlock lifetime access via a one-time In-App Purchase ($2.99 early bird through October 31, then $5.99 regular). There are no recurring subscriptions.

How do I restore my purchase on another Mac?

Open BarNest Preferences → License and click Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID. Purchases are synced across all your Macs.

常见问题

BarNest 在哪里运行？

BarNest 常驻在您的顶部菜单栏中。点击菜单栏上的折叠箭头（< 或 >）即可展开或收起隐藏图标；右键点击该图标或按快捷键即可打开偏好设置。

为什么需要授予“辅助功能”权限？

macOS 系统需要辅助功能权限以允许应用感知菜单栏图标的物理坐标，从而实现无缝的隐藏与刘海屏防遮挡保护。BarNest 纯本地运行，不含任何联网上报或按键记录行为。

7 天免费试用与买断模式如何运作？

首次启动即自动开启 7 天全功能免费试用。试用到期后，可通过 App 内购买（In-App Purchase）进行一次性永久买断（首发冲榜早鸟价 $2.99 美元至 10 月 31 日，之后为 $5.99 美元正常价）。无需按月或按年订阅。

如何在其他 Mac 上恢复已购买的授权？

在任意登录了相同 Apple ID 的 Mac 上打开 BarNest，前往设置 → 授权许可，点击“恢复购买”即可立即重新激活，绝无重复扣费。
