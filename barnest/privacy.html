BarNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

BarNest is built strictly around a 100% Local-First architecture. Your privacy and personal data never leave your Mac.

Data Handled by the App

BarNest does not send your installed application list, menu bar items, user preferences, or device activity to any developer or remote servers. It requires no online account and contains zero third-party tracking, advertising, or telemetry SDKs.

Permissions Used

BarNest requests macOS Accessibility permission exclusively to calculate the physical geometry and screen layout of menu bar items so that it can fold and protect them from notch cutoff. It never logs keystrokes or inspects window contents.

Contact

If you have any questions, contact us at support@kevinlabs.app.

BarNest 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年10月9日

BarNest 严格遵循 100% 本地优先原则构建。您的个人数据与使用习惯绝不离开您的 Mac 本机。

应用处理的数据

BarNest 不会将您已安装的应用列表、菜单栏图标信息、配置偏好或任何使用行为上传至开发者或远程服务器。无需注册账号，且不包含任何第三方分析、广告或遥测 SDK。

系统权限使用说明

BarNest 仅申请 macOS 辅助功能（Accessibility）权限，用于测量菜单栏项目的屏幕物理坐标与排列顺序，从而实现折叠隐藏与防刘海屏遮挡功能。绝不记录键盘输入或窥探窗口内容。
