BarNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

BarNest is built strictly around a 100% Local-First and Zero-Permission architecture. Your privacy and personal data never leave your Mac.

Data Handled by the AppPermissions UsedContact

Data Handled by the App

BarNest does not send your installed application list, menu bar items, user preferences, or device activity to any developer or remote servers. It requires no online account and contains zero third-party tracking, advertising, or telemetry SDKs.

Permissions Used

BarNest is engineered with a strict Zero-Permission architecture. It does not request or use macOS Accessibility permissions, Screen Recording permissions, Full Disk Access, or network privileges. The app operates fully contained within the standard macOS App Sandbox.

Contact

If you have any questions, contact us at support@kevinlabs.app.
